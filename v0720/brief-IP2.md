## Lead decisions for IP2
- **L33 form and length.** Keep 16 bars at 126 BPM, 4/4 (672,000 samples, 30.48 s) so in-game memory (D + mission family = 1,730,400 ≤ 1.8M) and build time stay as they are. Restructure the 16 bars as a song form with 4-bar phrases: **a** (bars 1–4, statement, half cadence) → **a′** (5–8, answer, ends more closed) → **b** (9–12, contrast/lift — e.g. the relative major F with a secondary dominant or a rising sequence, tension building) → **a″** (13–16, return with the climax note and the strongest cadence; bar 16 ends on the dominant (A or A7, a sus4→3 is welcome) so the loop pulls back into bar 1). Harmony may use half-bar chords, passing bass notes and suspensions; the chord list format may be extended for that.
- **L34 melody: motif-driven, chosen by a musicality score.** Keep the policy: no hand-written note sequences in source (the existing test stays), anti-cliché rules stay, no imitation of any known tune (軍艦行進曲 was only a tempo reference). Extend the composer so a theme can declare a **motif plan**: a seeded 1–2 bar motif (rhythm + contour) that phrases reuse with transformations (exact repeat, sequence by a step or third, rhythmic variation, inversion/fragment in b), a planned **climax** (highest note once, in a″ around bars 13–14), and cadence formulas at phrase ends (longer note, chord tone, 5̂ at half cadences, 1̂/3̂ at closed ones). Then choose the seed by **best musicality score over many seeds** (e.g. 200–500 in `scripts/sfx-music.ts SEARCH=1`, offline; the game never searches) instead of "first passing seed". Define the score in code and test it: motif recurrence (≥ 3 recognisable returns), contour arch with the climax at 75–90 % of the loop, mostly steps with a few expressive leaps (each leap ≤ an octave and recovered), rhythmic coherence with the motif, phrase-end strength, range ≥ a 9th and ≤ a 13th, no note repeated more than twice in a row. Report the top 5 seeds' scores and why the winner wins.
- **L35 arrangement and production ("名曲" is also orchestration).** Add what a memorable march theme has and D lacks: a **countermelody** (strings or a second horn, in contrary motion, longer notes, active in a′ and a″), a **moving bass** (passing tones between roots, not only roots), a **dynamic arc** (a mp → a′ mf → b crescendo with snare rolls → a″ ff with a soft cymbal crash on bar 13 → settle into the loop), and fills into phrase starts. Keep every fundamental of sustained notes ≥ 200 Hz (iPhone speaker lesson IP1/IP2 of v0.7.1), no bright/buzzy tables (IP1: < 5 % energy above 2.5 kHz per wavetable voice), peaks/levels: menu exit −24 dBFS ±1.5, menu UI ≥ 12 dB over music through the real limiter.
- **L36 keys and lab.** The remake becomes the game's title theme: key `title_d` (default `MUSIC_TITLE_DEFAULT_ID = 'title_d'`). Keep the IP1 version only for comparison in the lab under a separate key (e.g. `title_d_prev`), shown as 「D（前）」. Remove A and E from the title candidates and the lab (the user decided); keep any instrument still used by other pieces. Lab row 0: ［D（今）新しい D］［D（前）］［止める］ with clear sub-lines; heading updated (title D chosen, slow-mo A chosen; the lab stays for comparison) and still passing the S3 heading test. Memory: in-game ≤ 1.8M; lab total (D + D（前） + family) ≤ 2.8M.
