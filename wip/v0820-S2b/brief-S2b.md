## Lead decisions
- **L29 bit-identity.** The 11 other missions stay bit-identical in the world and HUD (ab-replay all 58 × 3 audio states, jp04base, jp03, hud-ab, us04 incl. noradar, us06, g2 OK 39 / DIFF 0). The textures of every existing key stay byte-identical: tex-ab CHECK=1 ✗ 0, with exactly two new keys in B (`tex-battleship-fuso`, `tex-cruiser-mogami`) — report the tex-ab lines that show them. us_05 against S2: the lead runs the us05 set with `A05` = the S2 site, so the us_05 world must also be identical frame by frame for the four scripted runs (they never detonate). The only us_05 world change allowed is the shorter sink of a detonated ship (L32). Setup snapshots without `-u`; `setup-us05` may change only if the art key is in it — say so.
- **L30 the two shapes (D-A1, D-A2)** in the existing ship-art pipeline: new `assets/capital-plan.ts` (≤ 160, placement) and `config/capital-art-config.ts` (≤ 120, detail constants; imports nothing), the part types `pagoda`, `casemate`, `deck` drawn by `assets/destroyer-paint` (111 → ≤ 160), the keys in `config/ship-art-config`'s tables (`SHIP_ART_KEY_BY_ENEMY`, `ART_TEX_DENSITY` 2, and whatever third table the existing keys are in), `TEXTURE_KEYS` and `tests/art-inventory` as the subchaser was added in v0.8.1 S2b. Boot: a new `generateColumnTextures(scene, data, spriteScale)` in `assets/destroyer-art` (the `generatePicketTextures` shape) that draws one texture per distinct art key of every mission's `transit.column` that has a capital shape — generated once at Boot. Use the enemy palette. The drawn hull must match the record × sprite scale (425.5 × 66.2 and 401.2 × 40.4 world units) so the picture equals the hit area; prove it in a test (the `art-dims` way). Art randomness, if any, uses a new art seed constant — never shift an existing `ART_SEED_*` (existing textures byte-identical). The four us_05 destroyers keep today's Kagero art (substitute; say so in the CHANGELOG). Silhouette keys that the player must read at a glance: the battleship's tall stepped pagoda bridge and 6 twin turrets (2 fore, 2 mid, 2 aft), the cruiser's 3 fore turrets and the flat aft flight deck with rails.
- **L31 the 'magazine' look (D-A4).** `core/ship-ai`, `mission-scene`, `mission-line`, `game-data`, `result-scene` are not touched, so do NOT route a new event through the Scene. Build a visuals-only owner `systems/magazine-fx.ts` that the world's art (`world-art` or `world-visuals`, whichever owns the existing fx pools) creates only when `w.destroyers?.magazine` is non-null (other missions: not built, no per-frame work). Each render frame it compares `magazine.detonations` with its last count (O(1) when nothing happened); on a new detonation it reads that ship's position, heading and length (from `magazine.log` + the ship) and plays from the existing pools: a large blast at the ship's centre (a larger variant of today's `explosion-fx` big blast — add a method or a size argument there; the torpedo-hit look at the hit point still plays as today), then 2 secondaries at ±0.3 L along the hull at +0.25 s and +0.5 s (today's `secondary`), and the normal sink stage comes from `fire-fx`/`sink-fx` as for any sinking ship. Pending secondaries live in a fixed-size typed array (no allocation per frame or per detonation; §9 warning 8). Add `'magazine'` to `ExplosionLook` / `mission-effects` only if your design actually needs it — otherwise leave those types unchanged and say so. Constants (sizes, delays, ±0.3) in `config/capital-art-config` (look values, not strength). Sound: only through the calls `explosion-fx` already makes (`blast_l` for the big blast, `blast_s` for secondaries) — no new sound ids, no edits under `sfx-*`/`sfx-music-*`, and `tests/sfx-rules` import tables unchanged unless unavoidable (then say why). A camera shake is optional; if you add one it must not need the Scene file.
