## S1's notes for S2 (verified by the lead against `entities/destroyer.ts`)
- **Must fix — withdraw margin**: `Destroyer.step` in retire mode sets `withdrawn` (and stops) once the centre is `boundsMarginM` (118 m) beyond the edge, i.e. before the leg's `goneX` (150 m beyond). Column ships must not use that built-in withdrawal while they are in transit (they would stop short and stay IN/OUT forever); the core's AWAY/GONE drives `goAway`. The cover keeps the built-in behaviour.
- **Order each fixed step**: (1) destroyers move; (2) `stepShipTransit(st, setup.transit, feelDt, w.stepTimeS, dawnLatched, transitShips)`; (3) for `entered`/`returned` place the ship at that leg's appear point (`appearX`, `laneY`, `headingDeg`) in the same step and `startRetire` with the leg heading; (4) for `arrived`/`gone` call `goAway`; (5) for `coverLeaves` retire the cover west; (6) radar: only when `radarSweepDue(r, w.stepTimeS)`, fill the targets (cover + column ships; `afloat` = not sinking, not withdrawn/hidden, on the map; kind SHIP) and call `stepRadar(r, setup.radar, geo, w.stepTimeS, boat.x, boat.y, tg)`. `geo.land` = `landLinesFromShores(...)` for us_04 (`[]` for missions without shores; the radar is only created for missions with a `radar` block). The view repositions its images when `sweeps` changes.
- **Fleet gone**: `allOut` and `allSettled` also require `transitPending(st) === 0`.
